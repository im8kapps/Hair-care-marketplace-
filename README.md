🛍️ I. Homepage (The Storefront)
The homepage is the main entry point and must quickly establish the brand and guide users to products.

Component Description & Functionality
Header/Navigation Bar Persistent at the top. Logo, Search Bar (prominent), Cart Icon with item count, Account/Login Icon, Main Navigation Links (Shop by Product, Shop by Hair Type, Brands, About Us/Contact).
Hero Section Large, eye-catching banner. High-quality image/video, strong headline (e.g., "Transform Your Hair Care Routine"), and a clear Call-to-Action (e.g., "Shop New Arrivals").
Trust/Value Proposition Bar Strip just below the hero. Icons/text highlighting key benefits: Free Shipping over $X, 30-Day Guarantee, Ethically Sourced Ingredients.
Featured Categories Visual blocks. Link to top-level product types (e.g., Shampoos, Conditioners, Styling Tools, Treatments) or Hair Types (e.g., Curly, Straight, Coily).
Product Showcase Curated grids. Sections like "Best Sellers," "New Products," or "Staff Picks." Should feature 4-8 products with image, name, price, and quick 'Add to Cart' button.
Educational/Blog Content Module promoting expertise. Latest blog post title (e.g., "Guide to Winter Hair Care") or a link to the main Hair Care Guide/Tips section.
Customer Testimonials/Social Proof Slider or dedicated section. Showcase 3-5 star ratings and short, positive reviews. May include an Instagram feed integration.
Footer Contact Info, Email Signup/Newsletter (prominent), Social Media Links, Legal/Informational Links (Privacy Policy, Shipping/Returns, FAQs).
🔍 II. Product Discovery (Shop & Search)
These pages are critical for helping users find exactly what they need based on their hair type or concerns.

A. Product Listing Page (PLP)

Component Description & Functionality
Breadcrumbs Navigation trail. E.g., Home > Shop > Shampoos > For Dry Hair.
Filtering & Sorting Highly functional sidebar or collapsible menu. Filters: Hair Type (Curly, Wavy, etc.), Concern (Dryness, Frizz, Color Protection), Ingredient Preference (Sulfate-Free, Vegan), Price Range, Brand, Rating. Sorting: Price (Low/High), Best Selling, Newest, Top Rated.
Product Cards Grid view. Image, Product Name, Brand Name, Price, Star Rating, Quick 'Add to Cart' button, optional 'Wishlist' heart icon.
Pagination/Load More Clear way to view more results if the list is long.
B. Search Results Page

Mirrors the PLP structure but with the addition of a clear indicator of the search query (e.g., "Showing results for 'tea tree oil'").

Should offer "Did you mean..." suggestions for common misspellings.

Must prioritize showing Products but can also display relevant Blog Posts or FAQs.

💎 III. Product Detail Page (PDP)
This is the conversion page, where the user makes the final decision to purchase. It must be comprehensive and persuasive.

Component Description & Functionality
Product Images/Gallery High-quality, zoomable images. Include multiple angles, texture shots, and lifestyle/model photos.
Product Information Block Clear and concise. Product Name, Brand Name, Price, Star Rating (linked to full reviews), brief product description (2-3 lines).
Purchase Controls Highly visible and sticky (on mobile). Size/Volume Selector, Quantity Selector, 'Add to Cart' button (primary CTA), 'Buy Now' or 'Subscribe & Save' option.
Detailed Tabs Organized information below the fold. Tabs for: Description (full benefits, usage), Ingredients (full list with key ingredient call-outs), How to Use, Shipping & Returns.
Customer Reviews Dedicated, searchable section. Overall average rating, breakdown by star count, ability for users to filter by attributes (e.g., "Dry Hair," "Sensitive Scalp"), and an area to submit a review.
Related/Complementary Products "People who bought this also bought..." or "Complete the Routine" (e.g., pair the shampoo with its matching conditioner).
🛒 IV. Conversion Flow (Cart & Checkout)
The goal here is speed and minimal friction to reduce cart abandonment.

A. Shopping Cart Page

Review Items: Clear list of items, quantities, and subtotal. Ability to edit quantity or remove item.

Promo Code/Gift Card Input: Visible and easy to use.

Shipping Estimator: Small widget to calculate shipping cost based on zip code.

Clear Call-to-Action: Prominent "Proceed to Checkout" button.

Trust Signals: Badges/logos for secure payment providers (Visa, PayPal, etc.).

B. Checkout Flow

A multi-step, clean, and intuitive process (ideally a one-page or accordion checkout):

Shipping Information: Guest checkout option (with option to create an account later). Address input with auto-fill/validation.

Shipping Method: Selection of delivery speed/cost.

Payment Information: Payment methods (Credit Card, Digital Wallets like Apple Pay/Google Pay). Security Assurance is key.

Order Review/Confirmation: A final summary before the payment button.

👤 V. Account Management (Post-Sale)
For customer retention and a better user experience.

Dashboard: High-level view of recent orders, favorite products, and account settings.

Order History: Detailed list of past orders, with ability to view details, track current shipments, or easily reorder a past item.

Profile/Address Management: Update personal details and manage multiple shipping/billing addresses.

Wishlist/Favorites: List of saved products for future purchase.

Subscription Management (if applicable): If the client offers a subscription service, a dedicated area to pause, skip, or modify subscriptions.
